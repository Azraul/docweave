# Morgo

Champion Morgo is a tabby brown male, son of [[cats/nanny|Nanny]] and [[cats/appu|Appu]]. He is the twin brother of [[cats/maj|Maj]].

Born in 2022, he inherited his father's tabby brown coat and went on to earn his Champion title.

He is part of the [[litters/nanny-appu-2022|Nanny & Appu 2022 litter]].
