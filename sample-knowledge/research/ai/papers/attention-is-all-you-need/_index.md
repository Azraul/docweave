# Attention Is All You Need

**Vaswani et al., 2017.** Google Research.

Introduced the Transformer, a novel architecture based solely on attention mechanisms, dispensing with recurrence and convolutions entirely. The Transformer achieves state-of-the-art translation quality while training faster than prior recurrent or convolutional models.
